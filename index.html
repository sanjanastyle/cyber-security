CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS

What is Cybersecurity?

Stay Safe in the Digital World 🔐

Cybersecurity means protecting computers, mobile phones, networks, accounts and personal information from unauthorized access, scams and cyber attacks.

Every internet user should know basic cybersecurity rules to protect their identity, money and personal data.

🔒

Your Data = Your Responsibility

COMMON CYBER THREATS

Types of Fraud People Face

Learn about common scams and how criminals try to trick users.
🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites are used to trick people into sharing passwords, OTPs or other sensitive information.

⚠️ Warning: Do not click suspicious links.
💳

Online Payment Fraud

Fraudsters may trick users through fake payment requests, QR codes or payment links.

⚠️ Warning: Never share your UPI PIN.
📱

OTP Fraud

A scammer may pretend to be a bank employee or company representative and ask for your OTP.

⚠️ Warning: Never share an OTP with anyone.
💼

Fake Job Fraud

Fake job offers may ask for money or personal information before providing employment.

⚠️ Warning: Verify the company before paying.
🎁

Prize & Lottery Fraud

Messages may claim that you won a prize and ask you to pay a fee or provide personal details.

⚠️ Warning: Don't trust unexpected prizes.
👤

Identity Theft

Someone may misuse personal information such as passwords, IDs or account details.

⚠️ Warning: Keep personal information private.

Think Before You Click

A single careless click can expose your personal information. Always verify messages, websites and payment requests.

CYBER SAFETY

Important Cybersecurity Rules

01

Use Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords for important accounts.

02

Enable 2-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your accounts.

03

Never Share OTP or PIN

Banks and legitimate services should not ask you to reveal your OTP or PIN.

04

Check Website Addresses

Check the website address carefully before entering personal information.

05

Keep Software Updated

Update your phone, computer and applications regularly.

06

Don't Trust Unknown Messages

Be careful with unexpected links, attachments and requests.

PROTECT YOURSELF

Cybersecurity Precautions

🔐

Protect Passwords

Don't use the same password everywhere. Keep passwords private.

📧

Check Emails

Don't open suspicious attachments or links from unknown senders.

📲

Secure Your Phone

Use a screen lock and keep your operating system updated.

🌐

Use Safe Websites

Check the website address before entering passwords or payment information.
